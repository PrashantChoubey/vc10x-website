Mission

The people who allocate capital make decisions that shape markets, companies and families. But most of what they know is shared privately, in meetings and side conversations, if it's shared at all.

VC10X exists to change that. We get fund managers, CIOs, endowments and family offices to explain, on the record, how they build a thesis, run diligence, size risk and decide who gets the money. Then we make it useful for the next person facing the same decision.

How guests are selected

Most guests are invited. We also welcome pitches, and screen every one for substance and relevance to allocators.

  • Decision-makers first. Guests make or shape allocation decisions: fund managers, LPs, CIOs, family office principals and the people who advise them.
  • Direct experience. Guests speak from what they've done, not general commentary.
  • Something to teach. Every conversation should leave a listener with something they can use.
  • Editorial only. Guests never pay to appear.

To suggest a guest, or yourself, reach Prashant on LinkedIn or X.
